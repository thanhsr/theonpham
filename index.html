About Theon

Living between English and Spanish

I’m Theon Pham, an English–Spanish translator with ten years of experience. I spend my days moving meaning from one language to the other, and my books are built from what that work teaches me.

Translation taught me that the hardest part of a language is rarely the vocabulary. It is knowing how people actually say things: which word feels warm and which feels cold, where Spanish needs one word and English needs a sentence, and which “obvious” translation is quietly wrong. That is what I try to pass on.

Spanish is a language I learned rather than grew up with, and I earned the DELE, the official Spanish diploma awarded by the Instituto Cervantes. I know from experience which parts of Spanish trip learners up. So my books don’t read like textbooks. Each page is a short, meaningful piece of English with real Spanish woven in, followed by a Spanish Note written for learners: idioms, false friends, regional differences and the grammar behind the phrase.

How I work

  • Natural Spanish first. I translate for meaning and tone, not word for word, and use everyday Latin American phrasing unless a page says otherwise.
  • Checked against the references. Grammar and usage are checked against the dictionaries and grammar of the Real Academia Española and the Association of Spanish Language Academies (ASALE).
  • Regional differences noted. When a word or structure changes between Spain and Latin America, I say so.
  • Open to corrections. If you spot a mistake in a book or article, email me and I will fix it. Read my editorial policy.
