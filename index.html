About

Stories, faith & the numbers behind them

I believe the most lasting work is quiet: a story that finds the right reader on a hard day, a sentence that stays with you long after the page is closed.

As the founder of Still With Jesus, I run an online home for real-feeling stories of answered prayers, faith through hardship and everyday grace. As an author, I write bilingual books that pair English with Spanish so adult learners can pick up a language through short, gentle reflections.

Behind the writing sits a practical side: digital marketing, content strategy and personal finance. I care about building projects that are meaningful and sustainable at the same time.
